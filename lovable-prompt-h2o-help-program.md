Add a new program card to the `/programs-services` page under the **Customer Service** category for Pennsylvania American Water's H2O Help to Others Program.

## Program Details

### Title
**H2O Help to Others Program**

### Category
Customer Service

### Description
Pennsylvania American Water has been assisting customers who qualify through its H2O Help to Others Program for more than 30 years. This comprehensive assistance program helps eligible customers with water bill affordability through multiple forms of support.

### Key Benefits (Display as bullet points or cards)

1. **Financial Grants**
   - Grants of up to $500 per household per year
   - Available to customers with household incomes up to 250% of Federal Poverty Income Guidelines (FPIG)

2. **Bill Discounts**
   - 30% to 90% discount on the monthly service fee
   - 20% to 80% discount on the monthly volumetric charges
   - Saves the average residential customer using 3,201 gallons/month between $17.66 to $64.99 every month

3. **Water-Saving Kit**
   - Low-flow shower head
   - Faucet aerators
   - Toilet tank diverter
   - Non-toxic leak-detecting dye tablets

4. **Arrearage Forgiveness Program** (NEW)
   - Forgives $25 of past due balance when customers make a current monthly bill payment in full
   - Requires a $5 co-payment
   - Helps customers reduce accumulated debt

### Eligibility
- Household income up to 250% of Federal Poverty Income Guidelines (FPIG)
- Available for both water and wastewater services
- Pennsylvania American Water customers

### How to Apply

**By Phone:**
- Contact Dollar Energy Fund (program administrator)
- Call: 1-888-282-6816
- Representatives available to verify eligibility

**Online:**
- Visit: https://www.dollarenergy.org/myapp/
- Complete online application
- Eligibility verification handled by Dollar Energy Fund
