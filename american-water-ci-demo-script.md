# American Water C&I Demo Script
## The Property Manager's Story

---

## Act 1 – Setting the Stage (2 minutes)

"Let's imagine **Hudson Property Group** — a commercial real estate firm managing over 50 properties across the region.

**David**, a senior property manager, oversees a portfolio of 30 mixed-use buildings — apartment complexes, retail centers, and office buildings. Every month, he needs to ensure water service is uninterrupted, bills are paid on time, tenant move-ins are processed quickly, and any service issues are resolved before they become complaints.

Right now, most water utilities force David to manage each property separately. Different account numbers, separate logins, individual payments, one-by-one outage reports. When a water main breaks and affects multiple buildings, he's on the phone for hours.

**Today, I'm going to show you how American Water solves that.**

With our Commercial & Industrial platform, David doesn't think in individual accounts — he **manages his entire portfolio in one unified experience**. Whether it's monitoring service across all properties, reporting multiple outages at once, processing bulk tenant move-ins, managing payments, or exploring conservation programs, he can do it all from a single dashboard with full transparency.

And as we walk through David's story together, I'll show you not just the screens and features, but the **business value**:

- How this **saves hours of administrative time**.
- How it **prevents service disruptions** from becoming tenant complaints.
- How it **streamlines operations** during high-volume move-in seasons.
- And how it transforms the utility–customer relationship from transactional to **true partnership**.

So let's step into David's shoes and experience how managing 30 properties can be as simple as managing one."

---

## Act 2 – The Dashboard (Overview & Landing - 2 minutes)

💡 **Pain**: No consolidated view. David has to check multiple accounts to understand his portfolio health.

💡 **Solution in Story**: Unified Portfolio Dashboard.

"David logs in, and immediately he sees his entire portfolio at a glance.

- **Portfolio summary** — total properties, active services, current consumption trends
- **High-level KPIs** — total outstanding balance, upcoming payment due dates, active service requests
- **Quick access tiles** — Outages, Payments, Move-Ins, Programs

Everything he needs to manage his day is right here. No more juggling spreadsheets or multiple browser tabs.

👉 **Pause & Engage the audience**: *'How many of you currently provide your commercial customers with a consolidated portfolio view like this? Or do they still need to access each account separately?'*

This is the foundation. From here, David can drill into any area — and that's where the real power shows up."

---

## Act 3 – Single Outage Report (2 minutes)

💡 **Pain**: Reporting outages is typically a phone call or separate web form per property. Time-consuming and frustrating during emergencies.

💡 **Solution in Story**: Streamlined Outage Reporting.

"David receives a call from one of his apartment building managers — **'The water pressure is extremely low, tenants are complaining.'**

He navigates to the **Outages** section, sees a map view of all his properties, and selects the specific building.

- He clicks **Report an Outage**
- Provides details about the issue
- Submits the request

Instantly, he gets:
- A **confirmation number**
- **Estimated response time**
- **Real-time status tracking**

No hold times. No waiting for a callback. Complete transparency.

👉 **Engage the audience**: *'In your current system, when a commercial customer reports an outage, do they get real-time status updates, or do they have to call back to check on progress?'*

This single-property workflow is table stakes. But watch what happens when multiple properties are affected."

---

## Act 4 – Bulk Outage Request (2 minutes)

💡 **Pain**: When infrastructure issues affect multiple properties, customers must report each one separately — wasting time during critical situations.

💡 **Solution in Story**: Bulk Outage Reporting.

"Now let's imagine there's **construction work that accidentally damaged a water main**, and it's affecting **five of David's buildings in the same district**.

In the old world, David would need to submit five separate outage reports — possibly spending 30 minutes on the phone or filling out five different forms.

**Here's what he does instead:**

From the portfolio map view, he **multi-selects all five affected properties**, clicks **Report Bulk Outage**, adds a description of the issue, and submits.

The system automatically:
- Creates individual service requests for each property
- Provides one consolidated confirmation
- Shows status tracking for all five locations in one view

👉 **Engage the audience**: *'This is the must-have feature for C&I and landlord customers. How much time and frustration would this save your large commercial accounts?'*

**This is what transforms the experience** — and it's the same workflow whether David manages 5 properties or 50. One action, full portfolio coverage."

---

## Act 5 – Portfolio View → Property-Level Drill-Down (3 minutes)

💡 **Pain**: No easy way to transition from high-level oversight to detailed property management without losing context.

💡 **Solution in Story**: Seamless Navigation with Full Property Detail.

"David notices one property — **Riverside Plaza** — has unusually high water usage this month. He wants to investigate.

From the **portfolio grid view**, he clicks into **Riverside Plaza** and immediately sees:

- **Service Overview** — account details, meter information, service status
- **Usage & Billing** — consumption trends, historical comparisons, current charges
- **Tenants & Contacts** — who's responsible for billing, maintenance contacts
- **Service History** — past outages, maintenance requests, billing adjustments

This is the same level of detail a single-property customer would see — but David can access it for **any property in his portfolio without switching contexts**.

He discovers there was a leak reported last month. He checks the maintenance tab, sees the work order was completed, and now understands why usage spiked.

👉 **Engage the audience**: *'Do your commercial customers currently have this kind of visibility? Can they drill down into individual property details while still maintaining their portfolio view?'*

This isn't just data — it's **actionable intelligence** that helps David run his business better."

---

## Act 6 – Payment Management (3 minutes)

💡 **Pain**: Paying multiple water bills separately is tedious, leads to missed payments, and creates reconciliation headaches.

💡 **Solution in Story**: Payment Groups & Bulk Payment.

"It's the first week of the month, and David needs to process payments for his entire portfolio.

In the **Payments** section, he sees:

- **Payment Groups** — he's organized his properties by region and property type for easier financial management
- **Outstanding Balances** — sorted by due date, with total exposure clearly displayed
- **Payment Methods** — saved bank accounts and credit cards for quick processing

David selects his **'Downtown Commercial' payment group** — 12 properties totaling $18,500 in outstanding balances.

Instead of 12 separate transactions, he:

1. Chooses his payment method
2. Reviews the allocation (he can adjust if needed)
3. Confirms one payment

The system processes it instantly and provides:
- **Confirmation number**
- **Payment breakdown by property**
- **Updated balances**
- **Receipt for accounting**

👉 **Engage the audience**: *'How many of your large commercial customers are still making individual payments per property? How much time — and how many potential late fees — would this save them?'*

And here's the strategic value: David can now **run financial reports across his portfolio**, track payment history by property or group, and maintain complete visibility for his accounting team."

## Act 7 – Programs (1 minute)

💡 **Pain**: Commercial customers often don't know what rebates, conservation programs, or budget billing options are available to them.

💡 **Solution in Story**: Program Catalog with Eligibility Intelligence.

"Finally, David wants to explore ways to reduce costs and manage his water budget more effectively.

He navigates to **Programs** and sees:

- **Rebate programs** — high-efficiency fixture rebates, irrigation upgrades
- **Conservation programs** — water audits, leak detection services
- **Commercial budget billing** — predictable monthly payments
- **Eligibility indicators** — the system automatically shows which of his properties qualify for each program

For example, five of his apartment buildings are eligible for a **toilet replacement rebate program** that could save him thousands annually.

He clicks into the program details to learn more — requirements, rebate amounts, how to apply. He doesn't enroll today, but now he knows exactly what's available and which properties qualify.

👉 **Engage the audience**: *'How many utilities proactively show commercial customers what programs they're eligible for? This turns your programs from underutilized budget items into real customer value.'*

This isn't just about saving money — it's about **partnership**. American Water is actively helping David run his business more efficiently."

## Pro Tips for Delivery

1. **Pause after engagement questions** — let the audience think and respond
2. **Use the persona's name frequently** — "David sees...", "David clicks..." — keeps it human
3. **Show, don't just tell** — actually click through the workflows as you narrate
4. **Connect features to business outcomes** — always tie back to time savings, risk reduction, or strategic value
5. **Adjust pacing based on audience reaction** — if they're excited about bulk outages, linger there; if they want to see payments, accelerate to that section
6. **End with confidence** — reinforce that this is industry-leading, not just incremental improvement
